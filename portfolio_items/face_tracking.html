Face Tracking System

Real-time face tracking and expression mapping using Intel RealSense SR300 and Radial Basis Function (RBF) neural networks.

Dec 2016 - Jul 2017 ADabisc Future Qatar C++ Unity OpenCV Dlib Intel RealSense Neural Networks RBF

The Challenge

Developed during my tenure at ADabisc Future Qatar, the objective was to engineer a responsive, real-time facial capture system capable of driving the expressions of 3D characters.

I selected the Intel RealSense SR300 camera for its combination of structured coded-light depth measurement and infrared (IR) sensing, enabling robust depth discrimination under variable ambient lighting.

Engine Integration & Pipeline

  • Unity Native Plugin: Integrated the Intel RealSense SDK into the Unity Game Engine via custom C++ native wrappers, unblocking high-throughput sensory pipeline streaming directly to engine memory.
  • Landmark Detection: Utilized Dlib and OpenCV to extract 68 facial landmark positions at runtime with low computational latency.

Calibration & Fast Training Pipeline

To solve non-linear mapping between facial landmarks and 3D mesh blend shape weights, I implemented a Radial Basis Function (RBF) neural network:

  • Dataset Collection: The subject performs a sequence of designated facial expressions in front of the RealSense camera. Coordinate displacements are captured and stored directly to disk.
  • Rapid Convergence: The RBF model trains on the captured pose dataset in 30 to 60 seconds, allowing instant calibration per actor.

Real-time Performance

Once trained, the system performs real-time inference, dynamically mapping live 68-point marker vectors directly to blend shape weights on the target 3D model with interactive framerates.
