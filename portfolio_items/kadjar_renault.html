Kadjar Renault AR

A sponsored mobile AR application showcasing the Renault Kadjar using Vuforia and Unity.

2016 - 2017 ADabisc Future Qatar Unity Vuforia AR Mobile

Interactive Car Showroom

Developed a high-fidelity AR experience for Renault to showcase their Kadjar model at promotional events. Utilizing Vuforia and Unity, the app allows users to interact with a virtual car in their physical environment.

  • Real-Time Paint Customization: Users can dynamically change the car’s paint color and wheel rims.
  • Interactive Hotspots: Explore vehicle features, animations, and interior details in real-time.
  • High Visual Quality: Tuned mobile PBR shaders and lighting for realistic reflections in diverse indoor environments.
